# Job Search Research

Terms used to consistently identify and assess companies for the daily job-search research process.

## Candidate Assessment

**Candidate Company**:
A company with an open role that is evaluated against the candidate's agreed target role, working conditions, and priorities.
_Avoid_: Lead, prospect

**Target Role**:
A Java/Spring backend or backend-led full-stack position. Full-stack roles include Vue and TypeScript work when it is complementary to backend ownership.
_Avoid_: Frontend-led full-stack role

**Target Experience Range**:
A role open to candidates with one through five years of relevant experience.
_Avoid_: Senior-only role

**Target Domain**:
Any of B2B SaaS, healthcare IT, fintech or payments, commerce or logistics, AI or data products, or another domain with a compelling technology and organisation fit.
_Avoid_: Industry filter

**Required Condition**:
A non-negotiable employment condition: Seoul or Gyeonggi location and permanent employment.
_Avoid_: Preference

**Work Preference**:
A desired employment characteristic that informs ranking without automatically excluding a company. Hybrid work is the current work preference.
_Avoid_: Required condition

**Priority**:
The ordered evaluation criteria for a candidate company: product and technical growth potential, total compensation, then engineering culture and code-quality practices.
_Avoid_: Score

**Compensation Target**:
The desired compensation is at least ten percent above the current compensation; the minimum acceptable compensation is the current compensation. Compensation is evaluated when a credible range is available.
_Avoid_: Fixed salary figure

**Recommendation**:
The research outcome that categorises a candidate company as Apply, Watch, or Exclude and states the evidence behind that category.
_Avoid_: Rating

**Excluded Area**:
The Geumcheon, Gasan Digital Complex, and Guro areas, which are excluded from candidate companies for this job-search process.
_Avoid_: Seoul-wide exclusion

**Company Transparency**:
The extent to which a company makes its business, product, team, technology environment, and role expectations knowable from credible public information.
_Avoid_: Detailed job posting

**Transparency Grade**:
The company-information assessment: Good when the company and its product or business are clearly described; So-so when the role is clear but company context is incomplete; Bad when the company or role cannot be assessed reliably.
_Avoid_: Application recommendation

**Application Fit**:
The assessment of whether the candidate should apply, based on eligibility, target role, required conditions, and priorities. It is independent of the transparency grade.
_Avoid_: Transparency grade
